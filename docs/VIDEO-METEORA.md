# VIDEO-METEORA: subscription launches on Meteora, under 3:00

- Pipeline: `~/Desktop/BRIEF-visum-video.md` and `~/Desktop/visum-video/`; fonts in `~/Desktop/interpres-video-kit`. Sergiu uploads to YouTube.
